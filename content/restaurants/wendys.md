## Free 10pc Nuggets & Junior Frosty

tags: free, rewards

1. Sign up for a new rewards account with TempMail
2. Wait about five to ten minutes
3. Go to the offers section in app and you can redeem each
4. For nuggets, you need to make a purchase to redeem the offer, so buy the cheapest thing on the menu

## Free Frosties & $2 Kids Meals

October only.

tags: free, budget, limited-time

1. Buy the Boo Book on the app
   - Gives coupons for five free junior frosties and one $2 kids meal w/ purchase
     - To qualify for the $2 kids meal, just buy another boo book
2. Can buy as many as you want during the month, expires at end of year
3. Kids meal includes small entree (cheeseburger or 4 pc nugget), small refillable drink, small fry, and toy
