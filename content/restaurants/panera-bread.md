## Unlimited Free Drinks - Sip Club Trial

3-month free trial of Sip Club Membership. Expired, for now.

tags: free, expired, rewards
links: [Sip Club registration](https://www.panerabread.com/en-us/app/subscription/1/registration-flow.html)

1. Click [this link](https://www.panerabread.com/en-us/app/subscription/1/registration-flow.html)
2. Click Join with Email (only works on new accounts I think but it might work on any account that hasn't paid for a subscription before)
3. Use your actual phone number and email since it lasts three months
4. Click create account
5. Make sure the $5/month for 3 months plan is selected
6. Scroll down to right under Payment Terms where it says Promo Code and click the pencil
7. Type in the code ~~3MOSIPS~~ Will update if new code comes out
8. The total should drop to zero, though you might still need to enter your card (make sure to cancel your subscription before it rolls over)
9. Once you are a member of the sip club, click rewards and then click redeem on your free beverage and select one of the options that appear
