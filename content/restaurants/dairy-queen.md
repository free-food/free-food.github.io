## Free Small Milkshake with $1 Purchase

Only needs an email (doesn't even require verification), no need for a real phone number either.

tags: free, budget, rewards

1. Add a small milkshake of any flavor to order.
2. Add two dipping 59¢ sauces (total $1.25).
3. Click rewards at the top of the menu, select free milkshake, and check out.
