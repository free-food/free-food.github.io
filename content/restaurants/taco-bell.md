## Free Food on Account Creation

Nothing required besides temp email.

tags: free, rewards
links: [tacobell.com](http://tacobell.com)

1. Go to [tacobell.com](http://tacobell.com) and make an account using a temporary email address
   - This is important, you must do this part on browser or else the reward might not populate
2. Once the account is created, sign into it on the mobile app
3. Go to the rewards section and choose your welcome reward
4. You can get one of the following for completely free
   - 2 (small) Chicken Bacon Ranch Chalupas
   - Beefy 5-Layer Burrito
   - Cantina Chicken Crispy Taco
   - Soft Taco
5. Complete the rest of your order as normal
